title: Grenette
description: Grenette was first released in 2020 and has frequently been used for humanist, eco-friendly brands and companies. Combining influences from Windsor and Richmond Old Style typefaces, Grenette shows a strong diagonal stress, imposing serifs and subtle quirks that give it a distinct flavour and a recognisable look in all styles. French independent media La Relève & La Peste use it in their logo and books, and I couldn’t be prouder.
