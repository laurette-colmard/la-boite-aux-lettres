title: L’Arche en France
description: L’Arche is a French non-profit organization supporting people with disabilities through multiple living communities across the country. In 2024, design agency The Imageniers was entrusted with their rebranding, Studio Méli-Mélo worked on the illustrations and I was in charge of the brand typeface. I led a two-day creative workshop with members at L’Arche, resulting in some characterful, peculiar letterforms and special memories. I went on to develop a lively and joyful font with multiple contextual alternates that celebrates diversity and uniqueness.
