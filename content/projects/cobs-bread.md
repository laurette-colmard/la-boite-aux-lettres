description: "COBS Bread is a Canadian bakery chain known for its freshly baked breads, pastries, and warm, community-focused neighbourhood stores. The custom typeface I designed for their new identity is based on the idea that the letters should behave like bread – the forms are not perfectly circular, instead they are heavy-bottomed, similar to how dough rises. The font family spans 3 weights and 3 widths with corresponding italics, but the wide styles shown below are definitely my favorites. "
