title: Cie Sylvie Santi
description: Sylvie Santi is a poet and artist exploring imaginary worlds and creating immersive shows with both oral and written poetry. We’ve been working together since early 2024, trying to give shape to her words through typography. Our first exploration includes a hybrid type system, that plays with forms and counterforms to create organic rhythms and to transform letterforms into creatures. The typographic duo makes for a very expressive, singular visual language. There’s more to explore and we’re both looking forward to it.
