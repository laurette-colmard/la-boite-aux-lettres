description: Established in December 1864 by Rudolf Wall, Dagens Nyheteris a Swedish daily newspaper providing comprehensive national and international coverage. In 2023, in collaboration with Göran Söderström and the team at DN, I helped develop an extensive serif type family, consisting of seven weights, three optical sizes and italics. Following Göran’s design approach of the roman styles, I designed the italics and developed Headline and Text versions. 42 fonts and a couple months, well spent.
